Next, we fetch data from our chosen river gauging station, Don at Parkhill in Scotland. For 10 years of 15-minute interval data, we are fetching over 350,000 records. To minimse the chances of a 504 Gateway Timeout, we will fetch the data in 1-year blocks and save it to our local disk so future runs do not have to re-download the data.
